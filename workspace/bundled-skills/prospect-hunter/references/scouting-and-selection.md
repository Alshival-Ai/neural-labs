# Scouting and selection

## Find a business that benefits from a better website

Use the requested city and the caller's explicit category or area constraints.
Do not silently widen the city or concentrate on restaurants. Keep the existing
search budget, category rotation, deduplication and minimum credible-shortlist
requirements. Never let generic stock-video availability determine the winner.

Favor a real, operating, independently run local business with a clear customer
action and a demonstrable gap in its current online presence. Exclude national
or regional chains, franchises, big-box stores and corporate branch locations,
even when the local listing has no website link. Confirm exact identity and
location; a business name alone does not establish independent ownership. Use
accessible official/about/location evidence where available. If chain status is
uncertain, record the uncertainty and prefer a candidate whose eligibility can
be established rather than claiming that it is independently owned.

## Screen the website opportunity

Check the listing's website link, the exact business name plus city, and any
already-discovered official profile links. Match the domain to the business;
a directory page, unrelated namesake or corporate chain page is not the same
as an independent business's own website. Keep searches within the caller's
budget. Record URLs checked, times and observed outcomes.

Classify the current presence:

- `no-official-site-found`: no matching official website found in the documented
  checks. This is a bounded search result, not proof of absence.
- `social-or-listing-only`: observed customer information depends on social or
  directory profiles; no separate official site was found in those checks.
- `site-with-demonstrated-gaps`: a matching site exists and inspection identifies
  specific customer-facing weaknesses.
- `adequate-site`: the current site already communicates its offering and lets
  visitors complete the main action; a new concept has little clear advantage.
- `unknown`: access, identity or tool limitations prevent a useful assessment.

For an existing site, assess the homepage and the main customer-action path at
normal desktop and mobile widths when browser inspection is available. Look for
unreadable mobile layouts, broken navigation or destinations, missing practical
information, unclear services, weak identity/CTA hierarchy, or visibly stale and
conflicting content. Use only what was actually observed. Without a browser,
report the checks supported by accessible source content and leave visual or
interaction quality unverified.

An old copyright date, older framework, unfashionable style, plain design or
single failed fetch is not sufficient evidence of an outdated or broken site.
Recheck an apparent outage through another accessible source before treating it
as a defect. Never claim a measured performance or usability failure without a
measurement or direct observation.

## Rank the credible shortlist

Apply exclusion rules before ranking. Among eligible candidates, prefer:

1. A supported website gap: no official site found, social-only presence, or
   concrete weaknesses where a new site would improve the customer experience.
2. A useful visitor action: calling, booking, exploring services, viewing a menu,
   getting directions or planning a visit, with enough evidence to justify it.
3. A clear local identity and enough public facts for an honest concept.
4. A plausible path to authentic identity assets or honestly representative media,
   based on available metadata; do not resolve, inspect or acquire photos here.
5. Diversity relative to recently selected categories, after business fit and
   opportunity have been established.

Explain the comparison in plain language. Numeric scores are optional; they
must not hide unknowns or substitute invented facts for evidence. Do not select
an already adequate site merely because the business is easy to design for.
If no candidate offers a supported opportunity, preserve the shortlist and
report the blocked result rather than manufacture a deficiency or widen scope.

## Handoff evidence

For each shortlisted business, record its exact identity, city/category,
independence evidence or uncertainty, website status and URL, checks performed,
observed gaps, likely visitor benefit, exclusions and selection/rejection reason
in the discovery records. Keep evidence separate from creative possibilities.

The selected handoff explains why this business is a good prospect and what a
better site could help visitors do. It does not assert that deep research, photo
inspection or production verification is complete. Those remain a separate stage
for the selected identity. Do not contact the business or test by submitting forms.
