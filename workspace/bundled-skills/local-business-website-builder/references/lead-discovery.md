# Lead Discovery

Use this reference when the workflow begins with finding or evaluating a prospective local business.

## Human-in-the-loop discovery

- Let the user select a candidate in Google Maps or another discovery interface. Do not automate scraping, bulk extraction, list building, or copying restricted listing content into project files.
- A missing website button or URL means `no official website found in this check`, not proof that the business has no website. Record the date and recheck with permitted web search and direct business confirmation before outreach or launch.
- Do not download, OCR, or transform listing photos, reviews, menus, addresses, or contact fields when the source terms prohibit reuse outside the service.
- Reverify useful facts through sources that permit the intended use: information supplied by the business, direct confirmation, an official business-controlled profile, a permitted public record, or another authorized source.

## Candidate record

For one user-selected prospect, record:

- User-entered business name and rough location
- Category and likely primary conversion
- Website check status and date
- Permitted verification sources
- Confidence and missing facts
- Whether the intended output is a private concept or authorized production work

Do not create a mailing list, telemarketing list, or lead database from restricted map content. Do not contact the business, send messages, publish a concept, buy a domain, or create external accounts unless the user separately authorizes that action.

## Stop conditions

Stop discovery and report the blocker when the only available source prohibits the intended reuse, the candidate identity is ambiguous, or the request would require bypassing access controls, rate limits, or source attribution.
