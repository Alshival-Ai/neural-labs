# Coordinated imagery for this workflow

Default order: suitable authentic assets with a usable source; then deliberately
generated representative assets from the recorded art direction; stock only when
explicitly requested or a specific documented fallback is justified. Pexels is
not the automatic next step after Maps photos cannot be rehosted. Do not discard
the observed character of the place when selecting representative assets.

Before sourcing, specify each role's subject, composition, customer relevance,
lighting, palette, aspect ratio, desktop/mobile crop and relationship to the other
images. For generated work make a coordinated set with the available image tool,
not independent generic prompts. Keep prompts and provider provenance private,
inspect output at intended size, reject anatomy/hair/tool defects, optimize local
derivatives, and preserve originals outside site/. Prefer clear photography over
images that falsely imply this business's staff, clients, interior or past work.
Describe representative imagery honestly and never invent results/testimonials.

Discover configured `image_generate` availability before choosing a fallback.
Record the result or failed attempt in an evidence file. Existing configured
generation within the task may be used; don't install providers, purchase credits
or configure billing. If it is unavailable, record that limit and make a strong
typographic/layout concept or justify a fitting licensed fallback. For video,
compare whether generated still choreography tells the story better than stock
footage. Specific licensed motion footage remains possible when its communication
job and the unavailable/unsuitable alternative are recorded.

In BUSINESS-VISUAL-BRIEF.json record assetPolicy.generation and stockExceptions.
In MEDIA.json every visual asset has `origin: authentic|generated|stock|original-ui`
in addition to the existing identityClass and source fields. A stock exception
identifies the asset ID, `basis: user-requested|generation-unavailable|generation-unsuitable`,
reason and private evidencePath. An 'unsuitable' exception requires a documented
generation attempt, not 'Pexels was convenient'. Provider-unavailable evidence
is distinct from a tool the agent simply never checked. Inspect the selected
stock for actual fit and retain license/credit. Apply the same rule to derivatives.

Keep this sourcing policy in one place. A saved automation's older generic
'Pexels or image_generate' allowance does not require stock; follow this current
default unless the user explicitly chooses stock for the task.
