# Style Translation

Use this reference to turn business evidence and permission-cleared visual cues into an original website system without copying another design.

## Inputs

Rank design inputs by confidence:

1. Business-provided brand guide, logo, menu, signage, packaging, photography, or stated preferences.
2. Permission-cleared official materials controlled by the business.
3. Verified category, audience, price position, location context, and customer action.
4. Licensed representative media selected for a private concept.
5. Reversible creative assumptions, recorded as assumptions.

Inspect accessible Google Maps business photos as visual research, following
[business-photo-direction.md](business-photo-direction.md). Translate observed
spatial details, materials, offerings and atmosphere into an original website;
viewing a photo for inspiration and permission to republish that file are separate
decisions. Record observations and uncertainty without copying a photographer's
composition or treating stock/generated imagery as evidence of the real business.

## Translation method

Create a small decision ledger with `observation`, `confidence`, `design consequence`, and `replacement trigger`.

Examples:

- A client-provided hand-lettered menu may support a warm display face, but body copy still needs a highly readable text face.
- A verified premium positioning may support restrained color, generous whitespace, precise typography, and slower motion.
- A fast-service conversion may favor bold hierarchy, compact navigation, immediate hours/location, and a persistent mobile action.
- Dense, colorful authentic photography may need a quieter neutral interface rather than additional decorative color.

## Required output

Before coding, state:

- One-sentence art direction
- Primary and secondary customer feelings
- Display and body typography roles with responsive sizing logic
- Color roles and contrast plan
- Spacing, radius, border, image, icon, and surface language
- Layout rhythm and section-transition strategy
- Motion budget and selected signature effect
- Which decisions are verified, inferred, representative, or pending client replacement

Translate this into reusable tokens and components. Preserve enough variation between business types that the workflow accelerates decisions without producing a recognizable house template.
