# RichBoys: process benchmark, not a universal template

The approved reference is https://richboys-barbershop.demo.alshival.dev/.
Its editable source, original skill snapshots and build records are preserved
privately under `projects/website-quality-upgrade-20260915/reference/`.
Read the relevant source/brief when implementing a similar composition; do not
load the archived Codex skills as competing runtime instructions.

## What made this build distinctive

- A concise art direction preceded code: neighborhood barber editorial, strong
  condensed type, warm paper/ink palette with restrained vermilion accents.
- The opening used giant typography above a bounded photograph. This was a
  deliberate composition and would fail the old mandatory full-bleed overlay
  profile even though the user preferred the result.
- Four coordinated generated photos had explicit roles: barber hero, low fade,
  taper fade and razor-line design. Their lighting and framing formed one set.
  Generated examples were disclosed; no false portfolio, hours, prices or reviews.
- A GSAP header and subtle hero zoom supported one signature Tedy sticky gallery.
  The gallery moved from grouped photos to an outward spread, central message,
  final hold and normal document flow. The user requested these effects.
- Ordinary service content and a selectable cut guide carried the details outside
  the animated scene. Verified booking-provider links supplied the conversion.
- Real browser review corrected composition, image crops, animation centering and
  mobile behavior. Narrow/reduced-motion screens received an ordinary gallery.
- React/Vite/GSAP and self-hosted fonts supported the design. The framework itself
  was not evidence of quality. Its original source must still be checked against
  Neural Labs' stricter no-JS/static-content and publisher requirements when reused.

## Transfer the decisions

A salon might benefit from coordinated detail crops and a style chooser, while a
repair service needs verified service coverage and a quote route more than an
image spread. A restaurant may need a readable real menu and reservations ahead
of a cinematic sequence. Preserve recognizable art direction, purposeful assets,
content-specific section composition and observed browser iteration. Do not copy
RichBoys colors, text, service categories, haircut images or Tedy into unrelated
businesses. Use authentic media where available; generated media is an option for
truthfully labeled representative roles, not an authenticity replacement.
