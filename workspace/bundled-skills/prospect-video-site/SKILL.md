# Website pipeline compatibility

For new requests read `$site-generator`, which owns orchestration and selects
`$local-business-website-builder` by default. Do not impose the old cinematic-media-first
profile, stock sourcing or a fixed video/hover pair. A named business skips prospect
hunting. Preserve any explicit template, geographic, effect or output request.

Keep this package installed: scripts/pipeline.py, candidates.py and qa-site.mjs
provide durable selection, evidence, QA and release gates. Follow site-generator's
workflow-contract for their current interfaces. The references here retain the
research, conversion, restaurant and publication contracts. Existing completed
release records remain authoritative; never delete them to force a new build.
Resume unfinished projects without changing their selected identity or history.

Automations retain exclusive locks, current run identity, schedules, model settings
and subscriber-based notification delivery. Do not contact the business or infer
new outreach authorization. Only publish within the requested output scope through
the existing deploy/demo-pi skills, after helper-generated QA passes.
